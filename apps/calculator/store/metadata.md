## Full Description
SmartCalc is a beautifully designed, private, all-in-one utility app crafted for everyday calculations.

### Key Tools & Features:
- **Standard & Basic Calculator**: Fast, responsive arithmetic with automatic history tracking.
- **Scientific Calculator**: Trigonometry (sin, cos, tan), log, powers, square roots, factorials, and mathematical constants (π, e).
- **Financial Calculators**:
  - **EMI Calculator**: Calculate monthly installments and view amortization breakdowns.
  - **GST & VAT**: Quickly add or subtract sales taxes with customizable slabs.
  - **Discount Calculator**: Determine final bill amount and real savings in seconds.
  - **Percentage Tools**: Fast percent increase/decrease calculations.
- **Unit & Currency Converter**: Seamless conversion across length, weight, area, volume, and temperature.
- **Age & Date Calculator**: Calculate exact age in years, months, and days, plus days to your next celebration.

### Privacy & Simplicity:
- No forced logins or account requirements.
- Isolated local storage: your calculation history stays strictly on your device.
- Dark mode & Light mode support.
